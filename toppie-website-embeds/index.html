(02) Why Toppie exists

The internet is full of customers. Finding the right ones shouldn't be complicated.

Advertising opportunities are scattered across retailers, marketplaces, and digital platforms. For brands, discovering and managing those opportunities can be unnecessarily complex.

Toppie brings supported commerce advertising opportunities into one place, helping brands find new ways to reach shoppers and grow.

  1. 01 Discover Find relevant advertising opportunities across participating commerce environments.
  2. 02 Launch Create campaigns through a simple, guided workflow.
  3. 03 Grow Measure performance and expand into new opportunities.
