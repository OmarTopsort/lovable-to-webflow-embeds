Answer

What is Topsort T-Platform?

Topsort T-Platform is a complete retail media platform infrastructure layer that helps commerce companies launch, manage, and optimize their own retail media networks. It combines ad serving, real-time auctions, advertiser tools, campaign management, analytics, attribution, APIs, and AI-powered optimization in one integrated system.

Answer

Is T-Platform an ad server or a retail media platform?

T-Platform includes ad serving, but it is more than an ad server. It is a retail media infrastructure layer for auctions, sponsored listings, display, video, advertiser workflows, reporting, attribution, and optimization across commerce channels.
