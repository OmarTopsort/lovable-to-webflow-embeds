How offsite works with Topsort

  • Commerce audiences

    Audiences built from real purchase and intent signals, not modelled proxies.

  • One campaign workflow

    Advertisers extend an existing campaign instead of rebuilding it elsewhere.

  • Closed-loop measurement

    Offsite exposure attributed against onsite conversions in the same reporting.

  • Budget orchestration

    Shift spend between onsite and offsite as performance changes.

  • Brand controls

    Inventory, category and creative rules travel with the campaign.

  • Retailer data protection

    Audiences activate without exposing underlying customer data.

Offsite should be an extension of your network, not a separate business to operate.
