01

Commerce intelligence goes beyond campaign performance

A great ad model answers: which ad is most likely to perform? A commerce media system increasingly has to answer more:

What inventory should be monetized?Which products belong in the auction?How should sponsored and organic discovery work together?How should budgets pace?Which seller should receive more opportunity?Where should the next advertising dollar go?

Topsort connects auction performance to the wider economics of the commerce business.

Performance is an input. Commerce is the system.

02

Build around your marketplace, not a generic model

Every marketplace behaves differently.

Catalog structures differSearch behaves differentlyInventory changesSeller economics vary

Relevance can mean something completely different in furniture, fashion, grocery, travel, delivery, or marketplaces with one-of-a-kind inventory. Topsort is API-first where retailers need control, while its commerce intelligence handles the underlying auctions and optimization.

Customize what makes your marketplace different. Don't rebuild the monetization engine underneath it.
