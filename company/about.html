Founded in Silicon Valley. Built for everyone.

The largest marketplaces built powerful internal auction systems that smaller retailers could not access. Topsort was created to bring that infrastructure to every commerce platform.

2021 Founded

Topsort starts in Palo Alto to bring the auction technology only a handful of Silicon Valley giants had to everyone else.

2022 Auctions

We launch our core auctions infrastructure and Campaign API, giving any commerce platform a real-time marketplace for placements.

2023 Series A

We raise our Series A and launch BIDLESS™ autobidding, so advertisers win without manual bidding.

2024 Formats

Sponsored Brands and Video Ads go live: four ad formats running on one commerce media infrastructure.

2025 Everywhere

We launch Toppie and Toptimize, and expand beyond onsite into offsite and in-store.
