In-Store Media

Most retail still happens in stores. In-store media connects screens and store signals to the same decisioning system running your digital network.

Talk to salesMonetize in-store

What in-store adds

  • Screen inventory

    Digital screens treated as placements with eligibility and pacing rules.

  • Store-level assortment

    Only promote what is actually available in that store.

  • Transaction feedback

    In-store purchases close the loop for campaigns that ran in that location.

  • Unified campaigns

    Advertisers buy onsite, offsite and in-store from one workspace.

  • Regional planning

    Target by store cluster, region or format.

  • Partner integrations

    Work with existing screen networks and hardware partners.

What retailers need in place

Store-level product availability
Screen inventory and scheduling access
Transaction data feed
Store or cluster taxonomy
Creative and compliance rules
Reporting expectations from advertisers
