Questions about Agentic Traffic Control Room

What is Agentic Traffic Control Room?

Agentic Traffic Control Room is designed to help retailers understand and manage external AI shopping agents, bringing together agent activity, catalog information, associated orders, sponsored offers, and access controls.

Who is it for?

Retailers and marketplaces that want to understand agent-driven shopping and give their commerce, catalog, and retail media teams a way to manage it.

Can I see the shopper’s full conversation with an AI assistant?

The information available depends on the agent connection and what it shares. Supported integrations may provide shopper requests or additional context. Other interactions may show only the catalog query and response.

How does it help improve my catalog?

It surfaces product questions that agents could not answer from your catalog. Your team can review missing details and approve accurate answers for future requests.

How do sponsored offers work?

Brands can fund offers for selected products. Retailers configure the offer and budget, make it available to eligible interactions through supported connections, and track associated results.

Can I control which agents access my catalog?

You can allow or block individual managed connections and apply rules for unverified agents or identified price scrapers. These controls apply to access handled through those connections.
