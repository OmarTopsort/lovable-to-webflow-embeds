What is a commerce ad server?

Ad serving infrastructure designed for marketplaces, retailers, delivery apps, travel and financial platforms. Unlike publisher ad servers, it understands products, sellers, placements, transactions, availability, relevance and purchase outcomes.

T-Zero gives commerce teams this foundation: sponsored listings, auctions, catalog integration, conversion tracking, decision logs and reporting APIs. No heavy legacy adtech stack required.
