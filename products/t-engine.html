Ad serving is only the beginning.

Most ad servers were built to deliver ads. Modern commerce media needs to decide which ads deserve high-intent moments, how budgets should pace, how relevance and quality affect ranking, and how to optimize for both retailer revenue and advertiser outcomes.

That is the job of an intelligent ad engine. T-Engine turns ad serving into commerce-aware decisioning.

What makes T-Engine intelligent.

Every decision balances bid, relevance, budget and outcomes, in milliseconds.

01Real-time auctions

Commerce-native auctions that balance bid, relevance, placement, budget and business logic in milliseconds.

02Budget pacing

03Quality score

04Attribution-aware optimization

Connect ad decisions to clicks, conversions, attributed purchases and revenue.

05Relevance controls

06Yield optimization
