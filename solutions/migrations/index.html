Key capabilities

Everything you need to build, launch and scale, running on one commerce media infrastructure.

  • Migration assessment

    We audit your current setup, campaigns, placements, advertisers and billing, and map it to Topsort.

  • Data mapping

    Advertisers, campaigns, creatives and zones translated with validation and per-campaign review.

  • Parallel running

    Run Topsort alongside your current stack and compare results before shifting traffic.

  • API-first integration

    Drop-in APIs and SDKs connect to your catalog, search and checkout without a rebuild.

  • Reporting continuity

    Keep historical performance and advertiser reporting consistent through the switch.

  • Risk-free rollout

    Phased traffic ramps with rollback at every step protect revenue and advertiser trust.
