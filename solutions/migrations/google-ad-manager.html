A phased migration path from GAM to Topsort

Phase 01PlanningTraffic on Topsort: 10%

Audit GAM inventory, ad units, campaigns, targeting rules, creative formats, and reporting benchmarks.

Phase 02IntegrationTraffic on Topsort: 25%

Choose banners.js, GTM deployment, or pure API. Create corresponding Topsort slots and campaign structures.

Phase 03Parallel RunTraffic on Topsort: 50%

Run GAM and Topsort side by side, gradually shifting traffic and comparing performance.

Phase 04CutoverTraffic on Topsort: 75%

Move selected traffic fully to Topsort while keeping GAM available during validation.

Phase 05OptimizationTraffic on Topsort: 100%

Tune targeting, reporting, pacing, campaign workflows, and performance after launch.
